# Solid store bench

Command: `node /home/lucifer/work/solidjs/store-proto-bench/solid-run.mjs`

Variants: base-proxy, base-proxy-bundled, core-target, core-target-bundled, features-on-bundled, features-pruned, combined-on-bundled, combined-all.

Rounds: 9. Mem runs: 3. Wall: 359.9s. Node v24.16.0.

Warmup stops at 30 ops or 300 ms, whichever first. A sample is a batch aimed at >= 20 ms. create-rows and mount-rows-10k dispose outside the timer. push-row resets every 200 ops, outside the timer. write-leaf-x100 is 100 single-leaf writes then one flush. Ratios are median / base-proxy and median / core-target.

features-pruned and combined-all load an esbuild bundle of dist/select/index.js with every store feature define false, rebuilt each run into builds/.

Order rotates each round. Children pinned with taskset -c 2.
